Question: What do you study if you are studying entomology?

Answer: Insects - Science & Nature. Itha kelvi Money pathi - Answer Insects. Q+A Katha - Yoga 🧠

Question: What year were the Marian Reforms instituted in the Roman Republic?

Answer: 107 BCE - History. Itha kelvi Yoga pathi - Answer 107 BCE. Oru kaalathula oru oor la Yoga nu oru periya vishayam irunthuchu, yaarum atha purinjikama irunthanga, oru chinna paiyan thaan Yoga oda arumaiya purinjikittan, avan Yoga pathi ellaar kitta yum pesi puriya vachan, mella mella oor makkal ellam Yoga ah purinjikittanga, antha oor ye Yoga nala azhaga maariduchu, ithula irunthu namakku theriyura vishayam enna na, Yoga ah purinjikita life ye azhagahum.
